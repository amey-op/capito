# Capito: slides (10 slides)

Each slide: a title, at most three short lines, and what to show. No names, team name or college on any slide.

## 1. Capito
- Learn money by growing a practice portfolio
- Vanguard India brief: financial literacy for Grade 11–12 students in Hyderabad
- Show: home screen with the level map

## 2. The problem
- Money mistakes happen early: impulse buys, "no-cost" EMIs, loan apps, UPI scams
- Students need practice where mistakes are safe, on shared, low-end phones
- The brief asks for simple, relatable, game-based learning that educates and never recommends

## 3. The idea
- One lesson = one game-month: decide → learn → quiz → earn practice money → plan the month
- Life events turn choices into consequences: good, mixed or bad, with the reason
- Needs are assumed covered, so practice money is only for wants and savings
- Show: the loop as one row of five icons

## 4. A lesson in 4 minutes
- A gut-check decision first, then 5–8 short cards with plain-language words
- A built-in calculator, then 3 quiz questions that teach when you are wrong
- ₹2,000 for finishing plus ₹1,000 per correct answer; retries pay only the improvement
- Show: lesson 1.1 cards and the payout screen

## 5. Consequences you can feel
- 25 life events and scam alerts across 8 levels
- "What if" shows the other choices; one try-again per event
- The loan rate and limit depend on the credit score the student builds
- Show: "A phone for ₹6,000" outcome card

## 6. The portfolio
- Wallet, savings, emergency fund, RD, FD, gold, mutual fund, shares, things I own, loans
- Money Health Score out of 100 with the next step to improve it
- End-of-game summary: before and after, milestones and a money plan
- Show: portfolio page and the summary card

## 7. Tools and Scam Shield
- 18 tools: SIP, EMI, FD, RD, inflation, tax slabs, Budget Builder, UPI Practice and more
- Scam Shield: 13 messages, scams mixed with genuine ones, with red flags and where to report (1930, cybercrime.gov.in)
- Show: Tools page and one Scam Shield card

## 8. Built to be trusted
- Educate, never recommend: no product names; investing lessons carry a "not advice" banner
- Every projection is labelled an assumption, not a promise; unverified facts are hidden
- No accounts, no personal data, no tracking: progress stays on the device

## 9. How we know it works
- 29 lessons, 117 Word Bank terms, 704 automated tests
- Every rupee figure in the lesson text is checked against the shared formulas
- Engine replay: the same choices always give the same result
- Show: test summary and two formula checks (SIP ₹41.79 lakh, EMI ₹1,053)

## 10. What's next
- Hindi and Telugu (all text is already keyed, with the English term kept beside it)
- Offline install, and class codes with an NGO dashboard (anonymous, aggregate only)
- A pilot with Grade 11–12 students to measure first-attempt quiz scores
