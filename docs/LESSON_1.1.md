# Template lesson: 1.1 "Income, expenses, needs and wants"

> This is the pattern for every other lesson: **gut-check → cards → tool → quiz → money → Plan the Month**. Copy this shape for the remaining lessons. English text only; Hindi/Telugu follow after the English freeze, with the English term kept beside each translated term.
> Reading level: short sentences, one idea per card. Money in this lesson is **practice money**.

---

## 0. Lesson card (metadata)
- **Level / month:** Level 1, Month 1
- **Goal of the lesson:** students can tell income from expense, need from want, fixed from variable, and see how small daily spends add up. They understand that in the game their needs are covered and wants are not "bad".
- **Time:** about 4–5 minutes
- **Unlocks:** Wallet, My Wants (student adds their first want)
- **Core terms:** income · expense · need · want (plus mentioned: fixed expense, variable expense)

---

## 1. Gut-check (decision first, hypothetical)
**Screen text:**
> "It's Month 1. Your family covers food, school fees and travel. You have ₹2,000 to use however you like. A pair of sneakers you love costs ₹1,800.
> What do you do?"

| Choice | Result shown (does **not** change your real money) | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Buy the sneakers** | "You have the sneakers and ₹200 left. Next month a friend asks you to a movie: you have ₹200. Joy +3 now, but little room for anything else." | mixed |
| **B. Save all ₹2,000** | "You have ₹2,000 saved. Nothing to spend on a friend's movie, and no new shoes. Safe, but a little dull." | mixed |
| **C. Split: ₹1,200 save, ₹800 spend** | "You can enjoy something small and still grow your savings. Nice balance." | good |
> **Bridge line (after any choice):** "There's no single right answer. Let's learn the words that help you decide."

---

## 2. Cards (8). `text` = what shows; `art` = hand-drawn SVG idea; `tap` = reveal.

**Card 1: Hook**
- text: "Money comes in. Money goes out. Where does yours go?"
- art: a wallet with arrows in and out.

**Card 2: Income**
- text: "**Income** is money that comes to you. Pocket money, a gift, a part-time job, a scholarship."
- art: a hand dropping coins into a wallet.
- tap → **Word Bank: income** (see §4).
- Quick tap-sort (2 items): "Which is income? Diwali gift from uncle ✅ / school bus fee ❌".

**Card 3: Expense**
- text: "An **expense** is money you spend. Some come every month, like a phone recharge. Some come now and then, like a birthday gift."
- art: wallet with coins leaving.

**Card 4: Fixed or variable?** *(mentioned terms)*
- text: "**Fixed** expenses stay the same: bus pass, tuition fee. **Variable** expenses change: snacks, outings."
- art: two columns with icons.
- tap: "Which one can you change more easily? Variable."

**Card 5: Needs and wants**
- text: "A **need** is something you must have to live and study. A **want** is something that makes life nicer, but you can live without it."
- art: a plate of rice (need) and a pair of sneakers (want).
- tap → **Word Bank: need, want**.

**Card 6: The grey zone**
- text: "Some things are in between. A phone for studying is a need. A ₹80,000 phone is more of a want. Ask: 'What happens if I don't buy this?'"
- art: a phone with a slider between "need" and "want".
- mini tap-sort (6 items): *school fees (need), rice and dal (need), game skin ₹300 (want), branded sneakers (want), phone recharge for online class (need), movie with friends (want)*; instant feedback.

**Card 7: How this game works (the golden assumption)**
- text: "In Capito, your **needs are already covered** by your family or by you. The money you earn here is only for **wants and savings**."
- art: a house with a shield (needs covered) and a small wallet beside it.
- footer: "Wants are not bad. They just need a plan."

**Card 8: Small spends add up**
- text: "₹40 a day on snacks sounds small. In a year it's ₹14,600." *(40 × 365)*
- art: coins stacking into a tower, labelled "1 year".
- Opens **Leak Finder** (see §3).

---

## 3. Tool: **Leak Finder**
- **Inputs:** up to 5 rows: item name, ₹ per day (or per week), days per week. Presets: *chai*, *snacks*, *data pack*, *cold drink*, *game top-up*.
- **Output:** "Per year: ₹X" for each row and a total; a bar showing "that could have been" a goal item (e.g., "≈ a ₹3,000 laptop bag, 4 times").
- **Compute:** yearly = amount × times per year. Example `₹40 × 365 = ₹14,600` [CALC].
- **Footer:** "Practice numbers. Nothing here is advice."
- **Test cases:** (40/day) = 14,600 · (₹100/week × 52) = 5,200 · (₹0) = 0.

## 4. Word Bank cards (three layers; English term stays beside translated text)

| Term | 1. Say it simply | 2. See it in life | 3. Say the real word |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Income** | Money that comes to you. | "Your uncle gives ₹500 at Diwali; that's money in." | **Income** = money you receive, from pocket money, a job, a gift or a scholarship. |
| **Expense** | Money you spend. | "Ravi's phone recharge every month." | **Expense** = money that goes out. *Fixed*: same each time. *Variable*: changes. |
| **Need** | Something you must have to live and study. | "Rice, school fees, the bus." | **Need** = an essential. |
| **Want** | Something nice you could live without. | "Branded sneakers, a movie with friends." | **Want** = a non-essential you'd like. Wants are fine when planned. |

*Mentioned only (one line):* fixed expense, variable expense.

---

## 5. Quiz (3 questions, first attempt pays: ₹1,000 each)

**Q1 (tap one)** "Which of these is a **want**?"
- School fees · Rice at home · A ₹300 game skin · Bus pass
- ✅ *A ₹300 game skin.* Feedback if wrong: "Fees, rice and a bus pass are things you need to live and study. The skin is nice, but you can live without it."

**Q2 (tap one)** "You spend ₹40 on snacks every day. About how much is that in a year?"
- ₹4,000 · ₹14,600 · ₹40,000
- ✅ *₹14,600* (40 × 365). Wrong: "Multiply ₹40 by 365 days. Small spends add up."

**Q3 (true/false)** "Spending on wants is always a bad thing."
- ✅ *False.* Feedback: "Wants are part of a good life. The skill is planning for them, so they don't crowd out your savings."

**Payout:** ₹2,000 base + ₹1,000 × correct answers. A retry pays only the improvement.

---

## 6. Plan the Month (Month 1)
1. **Money lands in Wallet:** e.g., ₹2,000 + ₹3,000 (3/3 correct) = ₹5,000.
2. **Add your first want** (name, price, optional month; item or experience). *Example chips:* "Headphones ₹1,500", "Birthday trip ₹2,000", "Movie ₹300".
3. Decide: spend on a want, earmark money toward it, or keep in Wallet. *(Savings buckets don't exist yet; the default advice card says "Level 2 will give your savings a safe home.")*
4. **Month closes.** Month counter → 2.
5. No life event this month (events appear at the end of Level 1).

**Portfolio effect for this lesson:** Wallet created; My Wants created; Joy +1 if a want was bought; milestone "First step" for completing a lesson.
