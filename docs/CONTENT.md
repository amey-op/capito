# Rupee Quest: Content & Curriculum (v3)

> Companion to **MECHANICS.md** (game rules, money, clock, scoring, consequences, event tables) and **PLAN.md** (product, tech, split, timeline). A fully written template lesson is in **LESSON_1.1.md**.
> Tags: **[DECIDE]** your choice · **[VERIFY]** confirm on an official source before it appears on screen · **[CALC]** hand-computed, engine recomputes and tests it.
> Tiers: ★ MUST · ☆ SHOULD. **Level 9 and 4.3 are now MUST** (final quiz is our learning evidence; credit score drives loan offers).

---

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

Learning = growing your own portfolio. Every sub-section is **one game-month**: a **gut-check choice** first, then 5–8 cards, a mini-tool, a 3-question quiz that **pays practice money (₹2,000 + ₹1,000 per correct answer)**, then a **Plan the Month** step where the student spends on wants, saves toward goals or invests. Levels unlock buckets in a sensible order, and every level ends with **life events where each choice has a visible good or bad consequence** (see MECHANICS §7). Students can add their **own wants** any time and promote them to goals.

**The golden assumption:** *needs are already covered by family or by you; practice money is only for wants and savings.* Needs are taught but never simulated (MECHANICS §0).

## 3. The curriculum: 9 levels, 29 sub-sections (23 MUST)

Each sub-section: **Gut-check** (decision-first, hypothetical, shows its consequence, never touches your money) → **Cards** (key ideas) → **Tool** → **Quiz (3)** → **Plan the Month**. "Portfolio effect" = the one real decision the lesson adds. Core terms in **bold**.

### LEVEL 1: Money Basics · "Where does my money go?" (months 1–4)
*Unlocks: Wallet, My Wants/Goals, Net-worth card, Things I own.*

**★ 1.1 Income, expenses, needs and wants**
- Ideas: **income** vs **expense**; **fixed vs variable**; **need vs want** and the grey zone (a phone is a need, a ₹80,000 phone is a want); small daily spends add up: ₹40 a day ≈ ₹14,600 a year [CALC]; **in the game your needs are covered, your practice money is for wants and savings**; wants are not bad, they need a plan.
- Gut-check: "You get ₹2,000 this month; family covers food, fees and travel. Sneakers cost ₹1,800. Buy / save all / split?" (shows the trade-off).
- Tool: **Leak Finder** (enter a day's small spends, see the yearly total).
- Portfolio effect: Month 1 money lands in **Wallet**; student **adds their first want** to My Wants.
- Template lesson: see LESSON_1.1.md.

**★ 1.2 Turn wants into goals**
- Ideas: a want becomes a **goal** with an **amount** and a **deadline (in game-months)**; short/medium/long term; **opportunity cost**; monthly target = amount ÷ months [CALC] ₹3,000 in 6 months = ₹500/month; starting late raises it. **You can add or change wants and goals any time.**
- Gut-check: "Your friend wants you to join a ₹1,200 trip in 2 months. You have a ₹3,000 laptop-bag goal. What happens to each?"
- Tool: **Goal Planner** (amount, months → monthly target; no returns yet).
- Portfolio effect: student **sets 3 starter goals**; Goals tab shows progress bars; each month's money can be earmarked.

**★ 1.3 The budget: pay yourself first**
- Ideas: `Income − Savings = Spending`; **50-30-20** as a guide **and how it maps to the game**: with 50% needs already covered, the **30:20 left = 60% wants / 40% savings** [CALC] ₹4,000 → ₹2,400 wants, ₹1,600 savings; adjust to your life; even ₹100 saved first builds the habit; ways to track (notebook, UPI history, envelopes).
- Gut-check: "₹4,000 arrives. Spend first and save what's left, or save first? Fast-forward 3 months to see both."
- Tool: **Budget Builder** (two sliders: wants / savings; a surprise expense appears).
- Portfolio effect: the **Plan the Month** screen defaults to the **60/40 split**; the **savings rate** starts feeding the Health Score.

**★ 1.4 Assets, liabilities, net worth and depreciation**
- Ideas: **asset** vs **liability**; **net worth = assets − liabilities** [CALC] ₹60,000 − ₹10,000 = ₹50,000; some things lose value (**depreciation**), others tend to hold or grow (no guarantees); phone bought on EMI = paying interest on something losing value: ₹6,000 phone at 30%/yr ≈ ₹5,020 after 6 months [CALC].
- Gut-check: "You can buy a ₹6,000 phone or put ₹6,000 in a savings account. One year later, which is worth more?" 
- Tool: **Depreciation Slider**.
- Portfolio effect: the **net-worth card goes live**; items bought from wants appear in **Things I own** and lose value monthly.

**Level 1 events (month 4):** festival sale, friend's birthday, surprise ₹1,500 gift (MECHANICS §7).

### LEVEL 2: Banking & Digital Money · "Your money's safe home" (months 5–7)
*Unlocks: Savings account.*

**★ 2.1 Bank accounts and the words on your passbook**
- Ideas: savings vs current vs joint vs **minor/student account**; **KYC**, **nominee**, **IFSC**, **statement**, **interest**, **minimum balance** and charges; deposits are insured up to **₹5 lakh per depositor per bank** (principal + interest) **[VERIFY dicgc.org.in]**.
- Gut-check: "₹3,000 gift: keep in the drawer, put in a bank, or spend?" 
- Tool: **Passbook Reader** *(static labelled image if short on time)*.
- Portfolio effect: **Savings account** opens (3% p.a., +0.25% a month). Starting Profile changes this lesson's wording (e.g. "open your first account" vs "use it better").

**★ 2.2 Cards, UPI and the safe-payment rules**
- Ideas: **debit** (your money) vs **credit** (bank's money, you repay) vs prepaid; **UPI**; **UPI PIN is only for sending money**; **OTP** and **CVV** never shared; collect-request and QR tricks.
- Gut-check: "A message says a refund is waiting, enter your PIN to receive. What do you do?"
- Tool: **UPI Practice Mode** (fake screens: send vs receive).
- Portfolio effect: first **Scam Alert** appears; **shield meter** starts.

**☆ 2.3 How banks use your money**
- Ideas: deposits → loans; why banks pay interest; **neutral comparison of accounts** (interest vs minimum balance vs charges).
- Gut-check: "Account A: no minimum, 2.5%. Account B: ₹5,000 minimum, 3.5%. Which suits ₹3,000?"
- Portfolio effect: choose which account holds your savings (fees vs interest shown).

**Level 2 events:** cash at home vs bank, UPI scam alert, two accounts.

### LEVEL 3: Safety Net & Saving Habit · "Before you grow money, protect it" (months 8–10)
*Unlocks: Emergency fund, RD.*

**★ 3.1 The emergency fund**
- Ideas: **emergency fund**, **liquidity**, **buffer**; adult guide 3–6 months of expenses; **in the game 1 month = ₹3,000 reference need** (assumption, MECHANICS §0); where it can sit (savings account/liquid) and why not locked or risky; shocks (screen breaks, illness, lost wage).
- Gut-check: "A ₹2,800 phone repair arrives. You have ₹500 saved. What do you do?"
- Tool: **Months-Covered Meter**.
- Portfolio effect: **Emergency fund** bucket + meter feed the Health Score.

**☆ 3.2 Insurance in plain words**
- Ideas: **premium** for protection from a big loss; **health insurance**, **term insurance**, **endowment/money-back** compared (what each pays, what it costs, who it suits); **claim**; **disclosure**; **nominee**. Neutral wording: "Term plans pay only if the insured person dies; endowment plans also pay a maturity amount but usually cost more for the same cover."
- Gut-check: "A ₹60,000 hospital bill hits. With vs without insurance?"
- Tool: **Shock-Absorber** (bill with vs without insurance).
- Portfolio effect: **buy or skip health insurance** (adds a monthly premium of ₹150; reduces illness cost to ₹500 co-pay).

**★ 3.3 Recurring deposit: the saving-habit machine**
- Ideas: **RD** = a fixed amount every month for a fixed time; **instalment**, **maturity**, **premature closure**; interest, taxes later; good for goals with a date, **not** for emergencies (locked); [CALC] ₹500 × 6 months ≈ ₹3,057 at maturity.
- Gut-check: "Lock ₹500 a month for 6 months at ~6.5%, or keep it all in your wallet?"
- Tool: **RD Calculator** (config calculator).
- Portfolio effect: **RD bucket** opens; first **instalment auto-deducts** each month in Plan the Month.

**Level 3 events:** phone screen breaks, illness, RD instalment clash.

### LEVEL 4: Borrowing & Credit · "The real price of 'easy'" (months 11–14)
*Unlocks: Liabilities, credit profile (starts at 650 on first credit action).*

**★ 4.1 Loans and EMIs**
- Ideas: **principal, interest rate, tenure, EMI**; **total paid vs price today**; [CALC] ₹6,000 phone, 6 months at 18% → EMI ≈ ₹1,053, total ≈ ₹6,319, ≈ ₹319 extra; **flat vs reducing** ("flat 9% for a year" ≈ about 16% a year on the declining balance [CALC, approximate]); **processing fee**; **no-cost EMI** (often not free: the cost can sit in a lost discount or fees; compare total paid with the cash price).
- Gut-check: "A ₹6,000 phone: 'no-cost' 6 × ₹1,000, or ₹5,500 cash if you wait 3 months?" 
- Tool: **EMI & Total-Cost Calculator**.
- Portfolio effect: taking a loan adds a **liability** and an **EMI** that auto-deducts monthly.

**★ 4.2 Credit cards and Buy-Now-Pay-Later**
- Ideas: **credit limit**, **billing cycle**, **interest-free period**, **minimum due** (paying only the minimum leaves a large balance growing at a high rate **[VERIFY a current range before quoting a number]**), **late fee**, BNPL is still borrowing. Eligibility rules vary by issuer **[VERIFY, no source found]**.
- Gut-check: "You owe ₹4,000 on a card. Pay ₹200 minimum or the full amount?"
- Tool: **Minimum-Due Trap** *(static animation if short on time)*.
- Portfolio effect: accept or decline a card limit; **utilisation** feeds the credit score.

**★ 4.3 Credit score** *(promoted: it drives loan offers)*
- Ideas: a number from **300 to 900** showing how reliably you repay; **750+** is commonly treated as good (bands vary by source **[VERIFY]**); what moves it: paying on time, **utilisation**, missed payments, many applications; **your score decides the rate and the amount a lender offers** (MECHANICS §5 table).
- Gut-check: "Two students ask for the same ₹6,000 loan: score 760 and score 590. What does each get?"
- Tool: **Score Simulator** (toggle on-time/missed payments; illustrative).
- Portfolio effect: **credit score meter** appears; **later loan offers change with it** (rate and maximum).

**☆ 4.4 Formal vs informal borrowing**
- Ideas: bank/NBFC (rules, receipts) vs **moneylender / loan apps / chit** (very high effective rates, no paper trail, harassment); **check a lender is regulated** (RBI directory of digital lending apps; RBI Sachet to report); education loans exist, read the terms **[VERIFY details]**; **co-signing** means you owe if they don't.
- Gut-check: "A friend asks you to co-sign a ₹5,000 loan. What are you agreeing to?"
- Portfolio effect: choose a lender (bank vs app) for the next loan, with the total cost shown.

**Level 4 events:** phone for ₹6,000, co-sign request, instant-loan app.

### LEVEL 5: Growth I · "Why money must grow" (months 15–17)
*Unlocks: FD.*

**★ 5.1 Inflation**
- Ideas: **inflation**, **purchasing power**, **Rule of 70**, **real return = return − inflation**; [CALC] at 7% inflation ₹10 lakh today ≈ ₹5.1 lakh of today's buying power in 10 years.
- Gut-check: "A samosa costs ₹15 today. What might it cost in 10 years at 6% a year?" 
- Tool: **Inflation Eraser** (config calculator + fading ₹100 visual).
- Portfolio effect: every bucket shows a **real growth** tag (green if it beat inflation, red if not).

**★ 5.2 Simple vs compound interest and the Rule of 72**
- Ideas: interest on interest; [CALC] ₹1,000 at 10% for 20 years: simple = ₹3,000 total, compound ≈ ₹6,727; **Rule of 72** (8% → 9 years; 6% → 12 years); **time beats amount** (use the correct comparison: same monthly amount, started 10 years later gives far less; do **not** repeat the "3.5×" claim for ₹10k vs ₹20k).
- Gut-check: "Same ₹500 a month. Start now or wait 2 years?" 
- Tool: **Time Machine** (config calculator, two start dates).
- Portfolio effect: choose **when to start your first SIP/RD/FD** (early vs delayed), with a preview.

**★ 5.3 FD and small savings**
- Ideas: **FD** (lump sum, fixed rate and tenure), **premature withdrawal** (interest drops), tax on interest, small-savings rates are **set by the government each quarter** (PPF 7.1% for Jul–Sep 2026 **[VERIFY current quarter]**); FDs suit short-term safety; compare real return. [CALC] ₹6,000 for 6 months at 6.5% = ₹6,195.
- Gut-check: "₹6,000 bonus: FD for 6 months or keep it flexible?"
- Tool: **FD Calculator** (config calculator).
- Portfolio effect: **FD bucket** opens; early withdrawal has a visible cost.

**Level 5 events:** ₹6,000 bonus, class-trip fee, "double your money" offer.

**★ 6.2 Gold and other stores of value**

**★ 6.3 Mutual funds and SIP**
- Ideas: pooled money, a manager buys a basket; **units and NAV**; **SIP**; **rupee-cost averaging**; **index fund** vs actively managed (neutral: *index funds copy a list and charge less; actively managed funds try to beat it and charge more; neither is guaranteed to win*); **expense ratio**; **Direct vs Regular** (neutral: *Regular plans include a distributor's commission, Direct plans don't; the fee difference compounds*); **SEBI-regulated**; **minors invest through a parent/guardian; rules change at 18 [VERIFY AMFI/SEBI]**. Fee gap [CALC] ₹5,000/month × 20 years at 12% ≈ ₹49.96 lakh vs 11% ≈ ₹43.67 lakh (≈ ₹6.3 lakh).
- Gut-check: "Market falls 20% two months after you start a SIP. Sell, hold, or pause?"
- Tools: **SIP Explorer**, **Lumpsum**, **Goal-SIP**, **Fee Lens** (all config calculators).
- Portfolio effect: **SIP/MF bucket** opens; choose a monthly SIP; **crash episode** follows.

**★ 6.4 Shares and the stock market**
- Ideas: a **share** is a small piece of ownership; **exchange**, **Sensex/Nifty**; **demat account** (a guardian operates it for a minor **[VERIFY]**); why prices move; **price ≠ value**; why single-stock **tips** are risky; money needed within ~5 years sits poorly in volatile assets. No analysis methods.

- Tool: **Price Mood Swings** *(static chart if short on time)*.
- Portfolio effect: **Shares bucket** opens; choose how much (if any); **tip event** follows.

**☆ 6.5 How each option behaved over time** *(reworded from "Goal Ladder")*

### LEVEL 8: Fraud-Proof · "Protect your portfolio" (months 26–28)
*Unlocks: Shield. Real practice money is at risk in each scam.*

**★ 8.1 Phishing and pressure tricks**
- Ideas: urgency, secrecy, reward, authority, small trust-building payment; **phishing**, vishing, **social engineering**, spoofing, fake KYC, UPI collect/QR, "digital arrest" (police don't arrest anyone on a video call), fake customer-care numbers.
- Gut-check: "A call says your SIM will be blocked in one hour unless you press 1. What do you do?"
- Tool: **Scam Shield** (swipe 10–12 messages: scam or fine, then why).
- Portfolio effect: each correct swipe adds to the **shield meter**.

**★ 8.2 Investment and job scams: the 5-point check**
- Ideas: fake **task jobs**, **guaranteed returns**, **Ponzi/MLM**, finfluencer/Telegram tips. **5-point check:** (1) registered with SEBI/RBI/IRDAI? (2) realistic promise? (3) money goes to a company account, not a person? (4) easy to withdraw? (5) can I explain it to a friend? More than one "no" → stop and ask a trusted adult.
- Gut-check: "An offer says double your money in 90 days. Run the 5 checks."
- Tool: **Check-it Tool** (paste an offer, tick five boxes, verdict + where to verify, e.g. SEBI Check, RBI lending-apps list).
- Portfolio effect: passing the 5-point check on an alert **protects your money** and adds shield.

**★ 8.3 Protect, report, recover**
- Ideas: never share PIN/OTP/CVV; official app stores only; **no contact/gallery access to loan apps**; **report fast: 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in; RBI Sachet for unregulated lending/deposits**; tell the bank; keep screenshots; tell a trusted adult; where to complain about a bank or lender (RBI ombudsman route **[VERIFY]**). Sextortion module skipped until India-specific official guidance is verified.
- Gut-check: "You clicked a bad link and money left your account. What are your first three steps?"
- Tool: **"You've been scammed, now what?"** step-by-step simulator.
- Portfolio effect: shield meter reaches full → **Scam-proof** badge.

**Level 8 events:** task job, "KYC expires" SMS, Telegram tips group.
